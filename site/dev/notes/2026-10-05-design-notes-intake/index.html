Sorcerer story

Keep in mind one of the goals of this citadel mod is to tie Minecraft’s existing lore together. Ex where did the end come from where did the end portal come from where did the nether come from. Why are there zombies and skeletons and giant spiders, those kinda of things.

Maybe we come if with explanations for the warden and the ancient city and the wither.

Maybe the ancient city used to have god trees and the god tree sapling was such out of an ancient city from under a wardens nose by the first king of maybe the chest plate was made from a warden or something.

Need to research Minecraft lore for this

Maybe iron golems and ocean monument guards were made by old sorcerers

Same with wardens

I have decided that since there is a guild you can join pre citadel that is related but pre citadel there should be an alternative option

This option will be to become a sorcerer instead of an acolyte

So rule one. Once someone has raised the dead once the acolytes can tell and that storyline no longer works for them.

here’s some more background on the sorcerers before we decide on the new sorcerer storyline.

This is all subject to review and change.

the sorcerer who got chased to the end wanted something from the tree. It is not yet decided if his motives were good or bad.

at the time overworlders had hear myths about the end but no one had ever been.

the sorcerer flees to the end to escape the overworlder tree king guy. Sorcerers at the time knew about the end and were sometimes capable of visiting.

at the time the end was one big land not islands. And endermen were a powerful magical complex society.

Our sorcerer meets a sorceress who is studying with the end people and has a child with her. She dies soon after.

at the same time the king with the bow tricks an overworld sorceress into helping him build the first end portal allowing him to go to the end.

she figures out his intentions and goes to the end first to warn the sorcerer.

we need to establish why she couldn’t just kill the king.

she warns the sorcerer and also has a child with him.

the king shows up and kills the sorcerer before returning home.

we need to establish whether or not the king knows about the children and what happens to the second mother.

The first brother is raised by the endearment.

the brothers war at some point and turn the entire end into islands and destroy the enderman society

generations later (we need to decide how these two sorcerers live so long) the 11th king shows up to hatch the ender dragon (we need to figure out why his egg is the last one. Maybe his ancestor brought it back from the end with him then during the brothers war all of the dragons died. Maybe the dragons were the endermans source of power similar to the targayeans) and meets one of the brothers. Im thinking it’s the second brother who is acting like a good guy to get information. The second brother kills the king then heads to the overworld to kill the 12th king and do other bad shit.

the tree ends up poisoned, the 12th king dead and the two sorcerer brothers trapped in the citadel together with an undead 12th king.

how does this happen?

maybe the second brothers plan is kill the last king poison the tree and destroy the kingdom and the first brother shows up to stop him and ties a seal around the cast to the ender dragon and the two brothers so that the seal can’t break until it dies, and that it can’t break unless they are both alive.

that last part doesn’t make sense, why would they both need to be alive?

I don’t know yet this is just what im thinking to make it so they don’t kill each other.

this is all background for the player sorcerer storyline.

Before I get into the sorcerer storyline I also want to establish something else.

I want the acolyte and sourcerer storylines to both require certain feats to progress ideally related to Minecraft achievements. I don’t think it has enough good achievements but these are the kind of things im thinking.

These could be related to mailer for example you maybe have to give him every weapon and armor or every potion or every book or something like that

for acolytes im thinking things like

Smith Craft every weapon and armor piece that is craftable of every type/material Wear out a full set of armor. Maybe nephrite armor. Now this stuff may be way to hard but I think would be a great one

Brewer Experience every potion effect

Enchanter Find or make a copy of every book type, and or hit achievements related to every type. Ex falling from build height to bedrock would be related to feather falling. Or make a perfectly enchanted set of armor weapons and tools including tridents bows maces and crossbows and fishing rods. That could be good

warlord. idk maybe kill a certain amount of certain mobs, or bring maelor a certain amount of mob drops. Maybe kill the wither and or warden. Maybe kill every mob type. elder guardian should probably included in here somehow. Dogs should probably be involved in this Something like, kill 100 of every hostile mob maybe. maybe have a pack of 50 wolves could be one. maybe break a nephrite sword and axe would be part of this. maybe break a certain number of shields could be part of this. maybe potions and enchants are more minorly involved in this. I think that when combined with soldier it should definitely have some stuff connected to this. like kill x number of bandits, or a bandit king, or create a group of x soldiers, or own x heroes, or x legends.

for the sorcerer storyline I have some things I need to establish first and some related things

first off I want that storyline to end up with the castle being transported to the end and being replace with a big hole and a giant end portal the size of the castle.

I am not sure if I want the sorcerers to be unanimously viewed as bad but im leaning that direction

Im thinking of having the player have to choose between the two brothers at the end and for the good brother to look bad and the bad brother to look more good and it to end with two different outcomes. Idk which brother I want to be the one to end up with the castle in the end. Maybe both but with different other outcomes.

maybe one of the sorcerers mothers ends up being part of this storyline.

maybe the brother you choose stays alive.

im thinking if you choose the first (good) brother he stays alive and partners up with you. And im thinking that if you choose the other brother he ends up betraying you at some point. I think both require you to kill the undead king but for different reasons.

We also need to figure out the early game part of this questiline. How does the player get into the citadel, what’s his motivation, does he interact with other sorcerers first, is there one big sorcerer he interacts with that ends up causing him to get to the citadel. Maybe that’s the second brothers mother. I think that would make the most sense. are there other sorcerers already in the world from the mod before this? How do they tie into this story. Will sorcerers have classes similar to acolytes? How many??

I’m thinking of a few possible sorcerer classes btw some of these may be combined also. And idk if I want all these to be necromancers as a base class or possible separate I’m thinking of trying to make the necromancer the base somehow so all sorcerers get the debuff and stuff

Weather Animals Bard Maybe something enchant or potion related

Maybe make horcruxes possible for some of them
