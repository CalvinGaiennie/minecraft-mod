Traps vs spawners (break rules)

PieceDuring assault (king alive)After king + claim
Tripwire, plates, dispensers, exposed redstoneBreakable / removable where reachable — many corridors can be safed mid-push.
Garrison spawner blocksBreakable once seal open — valid mid-assault sapper play; silences that spawn source immediately.
Static hazards (webs, ice, water, lava lanes)Mine/clear like normal blocks once in reach.
Inner gates / ward doorsClosed in defensive mode; player uses levers (post-claim setup) to open routes — not a global auto-open TBD mockup.
