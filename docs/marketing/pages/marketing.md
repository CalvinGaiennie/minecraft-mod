# Minecraft Kingdom

**NeoForge 1.21.1** · **Java 21** · Two mods, one world. Install **Armies**, **Citadel**, or both. No required modpack dependencies.

Recruit villagers into soldiers, then **blow war horns** to rally them, hold a wall, or charge. Storm a sealed fortress and claim a throne. Or pick up a **necromancer's wand**, raise the dead, and pay for it in hearts and reputation.

Design details: **[Dev documentation](dev/index.html)**.

## Why this exists

**Minecraft is solo by default.** You trade with villagers, you don't lead them. The "helpful" farms are usually machines you babysit. We wanted a world that fights back and people who fight for you.

**The dragon isn't the whole game.** Kingdom play and the **Black Citadel** matter **before and after** you kill it. On the surface you build land and armies; the citadel is a massive **overworld** fortress (sealed until the dragon is dead) with a siege, bosses, and a throne. Some story paths **lock others off** once you commit.

## Minecraft Kingdom: Armies

*villages, war, your banner on the map*

**Make soldiers from real villagers.** Unemployed villagers can become militia and ranked soldiers. They wear armor, hold the line, and die on your orders.

**Command from the field.** Craftable **war horns** are your orders: wake the camp, send troops to a spot on the map, guard a rampart, or call a charge. You lead squads in the world instead of babysitting one helper mob.

**Turn villages into territory.** Recruit, supply, and defend places you care about. Raids, swarms, and bandits scale with how much land you hold. Grow big enough and the game treats you like a kingdom, titles, pressure, and rewards included.

**Play your way.** Want Create and peaceful building? Threats can stay optional. Want conquest? Campaign against bandit fortresses and named enemies.

## Minecraft Kingdom: Citadel

*necromancy, siege, throne*

**The Black Citadel on the overworld map.** After the dragon dies, you can break the seal on a fortress miles from spawn and fight your way in. Traps, spawners, debuffs, and tight layout, not just a damage sponge. Kill the Corrupted King and his two bound necromancers, then fight over who sits the throne.

**Own the citadel.** Claim it, remodel the interior, wake royal stations, heal the Blighted Tree, restore the Hall of the Twelve Kings. With Armies installed, kingdom perks and citadel-side content stack on top.

**Necromancy is power with a receipt.** The wand turns hostiles into minions; each use costs permanent hearts. Hood and robe buy peace from monsters and cost you goodwill with everyone else. **Horcruxes** make you nearly impossible to kill, poison-immune, harm-immune, fake death and all, but each one locks away max health forever and closes the path to absolution.

**Optional story, permanent forks.** Train with the **Order of the Well** before claim, or walk the necromancer road, or ignore both and still siege the citadel. Commit to the dark quest and the Order closes. **Create a horcrux to gain great power and the game punishes you.**

::: future
**Citadel alone** is a full endgame mod. **Citadel + Armies** adds kingdom titles, shared relic perks, and citadel mess ownership.
:::
